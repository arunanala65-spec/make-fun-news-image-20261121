# The World in One Day — 21 November 2026

One image that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026's world news, drawn as a
newspaper front page. The masthead names the day, and five panels cover the
stories that led the day's news, across regions and fields:

- **Missiles land in Poland** — Russian cruise missiles and a drone came
  down in south-eastern Poland during a huge overnight strike on Ukraine;
  Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it a deliberate provocation, NATO jets
  were scrambled, and Poland asked NATO for more air defences.
- **France: cost-of-living protests** — a sixth Saturday of nationwide
  protests against the rising cost of living, with a big march through
  Paris and about 70,000 police deployed across the country.
- **Peru: magnitude 6.8 quake** — a strong earthquake shook the Andean
  region of Junín, with tremors felt in Lima, some 320 km away.
- **Denmark foils "plot to kill rapper"** — Danish police said they had
  stopped an alleged Russian plot to assassinate Ukrainian-American rapper
  Kyivstoner; two men were charged and Russia's defence attaché was expelled.
- **Gaza: allies urge aid access as floods hit** — Australia, Canada, Italy
  and the UK jointly called on Israel to allow unrestricted humanitarian
  access as flooding added to the suffering in Gaza.

The footer strip notes the day's other headlines: the EU's €1 billion in
military aid for Ukraine, record foreign investment inflows into India, and
KL Rahul completing Test centuries in all four SENA countries.

The image itself is `entry.png` (1024 × 1024, under 1 MiB): a flat editorial
illustration, with each story recognisable from its picture and its short
label, readable even at about 300 pixels wide.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies the image in this repository to `out/entry.png`. The program
uses only the Node standard library, needs no network, and is deterministic:
two runs write byte-identical files.
